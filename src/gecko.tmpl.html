Fluoddity CA gecko

 
 
 

Run

A disc of mass, 27 cells across, grows into the gecko. Every cell runs the same trained rule: Fluoddity's Fourier brain reads the trail, the mass and six hidden numbers through two eyes, accelerates, and pushes its mass to where it lands. A small paint head carries the colour along. Nothing tells a cell where it is.
